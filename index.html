A little context before your next shift

The fine print, in big print.

Is $ANT connected to the company listing under “ANT”?

No. $ANT is an independent meme about a cartoon ant. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Anthropic, PBC or any other company. The overlap in three letters is the joke. The only link is that $ANT's trading pair is a third-party pre-IPO token (see below), which we picked ourselves without anyone's involvement or approval.

What does “paired with the pre-IPO token” mean?

$ANT launched on pump.fun using its pre-stock pairs feature. Instead of SOL, its trading pair is the Anthropic token issued by PreStocks, a third-party synthetic token. PreStocks, not Anthropic, issues it. So the $ANT pool holds that token, and $ANT's dollar price moves with it as well as with $ANT's own trading. PreStocks tokens are reported to be unavailable to residents of some regions, including the US, so check your eligibility. What happens to the pre-IPO token after the listing depends on PreStocks' terms, not on us.

Does holding $ANT give me shares or IPO exposure?

No. $ANT is a memecoin. It is not equity, not a security, and not a claim on Anthropic, PreStocks, or any company's shares, revenue or backing assets. It is worth whatever the market decides, which can be zero.

Does loading the ant cost anything?

No. The “load him up” game is just for fun. No wallet, no payment, no on-chain anything. ANT carries it for free, as always.

Is this financial advice?

No. Memecoins are extremely risky and most go to zero. Only spend what you're fine losing, always check the contract address, and never share your seed phrase with anyone.
